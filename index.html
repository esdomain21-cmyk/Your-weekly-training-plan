🌿 Wednesday — Recovery

No scheduled gym session.

Optional easy walk and gentle mobility if you feel good. Keep it comfortable; don't make up missed workouts.

🚶 Saturday — Recovery

Optional relaxed walk or gentle mobility.

Keep it light so you're recovered for Monday. Rest completely if you need it.

🛌 Sunday — FULL REST

No gym. No catch-up workout.

Recover, hydrate and get ready for Monday. Sunday stays off by default.

Training notes: Rest 60–90 seconds between most sets (up to 2 minutes on harder compound lifts). Choose a load where the final 2–3 reps are challenging while form remains controlled. Increase weight gradually when you can complete the top of the rep range comfortably. Stop if you feel sharp pain. Cardio is time-based; strength and core work are rep-based.
